Privacy

How the site handles messages, analytics, cookies, and future advertising. Last updated: November 11, 2026.

Today

At the date above, Photo Skill Path does not display advertising or affiliate links, and the local build does not load Google Analytics. We will update this policy before or when those practices change.

Email

If you email Hi@photoskillpath.com, we receive the information in your message and your email address. We use it to respond, review the issue, and maintain an appropriate record of the correspondence.

Analytics

After launch, we plan to use Google Analytics to understand aggregated site use and improve content. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure interactions with the site. We will not intentionally send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

Before enabling Analytics, we will configure the required notice and consent controls for the places where the site is offered, and will update this policy to describe the live configuration. Google explains how Analytics collects and processes data in its Analytics privacy information.

Cookies

When Analytics or advertising services are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies. Browser settings can generally be used to manage cookies. Google also explains its use of cookies in its cookie policy.

Advertising

We intend to apply to Google AdSense after the site is established. Approval is not guaranteed. If advertising is later enabled, this policy and our Advertising Disclosure will be updated to describe the service and applicable choices.

Contact

For a privacy question, email Hi@photoskillpath.com.

This is a site-policy draft based on the current build plan. It should be reviewed and updated for the site's actual hosting, analytics, cookie-consent, and advertising configuration before public launch.
