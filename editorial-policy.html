Editorial policy

How Photo Skill Path researches, writes, and maintains beginner photography guides.

Sources

Photo Skill Path uses a research-only editorial approach. We prefer official documentation, government or university material, archival sources, and primary documentation when explaining technical claims.

Articles are written to own one reader task and one subtopic. They should point to related guides rather than repeat the same explanation under a new title.

Limits

  • No fabricated personal tests, purchases, photos, credentials, or results.
  • No universal camera, phone, or editing settings.
  • No product rankings or gear reviews presented as hands-on testing.
  • No current-price, firmware, or platform-algorithm claims as core editorial advice.

Devices

Device- and app-specific instructions can change. We label those limits and encourage readers to verify current official documentation for their own device or software version.

Images

We use original visual material or assets with documented permission or licensing. Illustrations and examples are intended to clarify a concept, not to promise that a reader will get the same result.

Questions

To report a factual concern, missing context, or a rights issue, email Hi@photoskillpath.com with the page URL and a short description of the issue.
