Advertising

Our position on advertising, future AdSense plans, and sponsored material. Last updated: November 11, 2026.

Today

Photo Skill Path currently shows no display ads, affiliate links, sponsored content, or paid product placements.

Future plans

We plan to apply to Google AdSense after the site is established. Submission or use of this site does not mean that Google has approved the application. If advertising is enabled, we will update this page and the Privacy Policy before or when that change takes effect.

Independence

Advertising, if introduced, will not turn the site into a product-ranking or gear-review publication. Photography guidance will continue to focus on observable choices, practice, and clearly described limits.

Sponsorships

If we later publish an affiliate link, sponsorship, or paid collaboration, it will be identified near the relevant material. We will update this disclosure to reflect the arrangement.

Questions

For a question about advertising or a disclosure, email Hi@photoskillpath.com.
